Herobrine Incursion (赫罗布林的入侵)

Welcome to the Herobrine Incursion mod page!

In this mod, Herobrine clones will spawn naturally during survival gameplay. They will possess NPC players by defeating them, turning them into members of the Herobrine faction. Your goal is to continuously upgrade your gear and fight against them. The clones will keep spawning, and some of them have the ability to summon reinforcements to aid them in battle.
