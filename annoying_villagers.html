Annoying Villagers Modpack (该项目已废弃)

This is a fan-made Minecraft modpack inspired by Annoying Villagers animations. It is currently abandoned.

It adds over 30 creatures that spawn during gameplay. If you obtain certain boss weapons, you can use their abilities by holding X, C, or Z. However, these skills lack cooldowns, which breaks game balance. Most creatures also have very low health, making them easy to kill. It is intended for entertainment only.

I stopped working on it because I lost the workpack and could no longer compile or update it.
