EpicFight: Better Bows (史诗战斗:更好的弓)

This mod rewrites bow and crossbow shooting in Epic Fight. It auto-loads arrows, auto-aims, and adjusts distance to hit enemies, adding convenience and making ranged weapons viable. Sprinting also triggers a slide that can knock down enemies or create distance.
